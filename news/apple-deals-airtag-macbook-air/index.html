Apple Hardware Discounts: AirTag 4-Pack, M3 MacBook Air, and USB-C Pencil Slashed

⚡ Intel Brief TL;DR
  • Apple AirTag 4-pack drops to an all-time low of $79 ($19.75 per tracker) on Amazon.
  • 13-inch M3 MacBook Air receives a direct $200 discount down to $899.
  • USB-C Apple Pencil hits $59, offering affordable precision note-taking for iPad users.

Retailers have rolled out notable autumn price cuts across Apple's most popular hardware essentials, led by direct discounts on Amazon. The standout promotion is the official AirTag 4-pack, which has plunged to $79, bringing the per-tracker price under $20.

For mobile professionals and students, Apple's 13-inch MacBook Air equipped with the TSMC 3nm M3 chip has dropped by a full $200, landing at $899. The configuration delivers 18 hours of battery efficiency, dual external display support in clamshell mode, and 16GB of unified memory.

Complementing the hardware sale, the USB-C Apple Pencil has been marked down to $59, making it an accessible accessory for tablet users seeking low-latency note-taking and document markup without paying Pro-tier accessory premiums.
