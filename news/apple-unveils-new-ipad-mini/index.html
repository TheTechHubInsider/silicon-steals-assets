Apple Unveils New iPad Mini with M3 Chip

⚡ Intel Brief TL;DR
  • New iPad mini upgrades to the M-series silicon for major compute and graphics performance jumps.
  • Ultra-portable form factor with reduced bezel footprint and all-day battery efficiency.
  • Available for order starting at $499 across authorized retailers including Amazon.
Hardware SpecPredecessor / BaselineLeaked / Expected Spec
ProcessorA15 BionicM3
Battery LifeUp to 10 hoursUp to 12 hours

Apple has unveiled the new iPad mini, featuring a significant upgrade with the M3 chip. This marks a shift from the A15 Bionic processor, promising enhanced performance and efficiency.

The new model adopts a sleeker design, reducing its weight by 14g to just 294g. Additionally, it offers improved battery life of up to 12 hours, making it more suitable for extended use without needing a recharge.

Priced at $499, the new iPad mini is expected to be released in early 2027. This competitive pricing and enhanced features are likely to attract both existing Apple users and newcomers to the tablet market.
