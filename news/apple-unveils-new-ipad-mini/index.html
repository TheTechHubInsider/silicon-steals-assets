Apple Unveils New iPad Mini with A17 Pro Chip & Apple Intelligence

⚡ Intel Brief TL;DR
  • New iPad mini updates to the A17 Pro processor with hardware-accelerated ray tracing.
  • Equipped with 128GB base storage—doubling the previous generation at the same $499 entry MSRP.
  • Supports the Apple Pencil Pro with barrel roll gestures and tactile haptic feedback.
Hardware SpecPredecessor / BaselineLeaked / Expected Spec
ProcessorA15 Bionic (5nm)A17 Pro (3nm TSMC)

Apple has officially updated its most compact tablet, outfitting the seventh-generation iPad mini with the high-performance A17 Pro processor. The silicon transition unlocks support for Apple Intelligence foundation models and delivers a 30% jump in CPU performance alongside a 25% graphics boost.

Crucially for tablet buyers, Apple has retired the restrictive 64GB entry tier. The new iPad mini standardizes 128GB of high-speed NVMe storage at its unchanged $499 starting MSRP, addressing the primary bottleneck of previous ultra-portable iPad generations.

Connectivity sees notable upgrades as well: the USB-C port throughput doubles to 10 Gbps for high-speed camera offloads, while Wi-Fi 6E integration delivers lower latency for cloud gaming and remote desktop streaming.
