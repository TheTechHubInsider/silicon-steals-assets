Nintendo Switch 2 Storage: Samsung P9 MicroSD Express Slashed to Under $48

⚡ Intel Brief TL;DR
  • Samsung P9 MicroSD Express cards dropped to an all-time low of $47.99 (256GB) and $99.99 (512GB).
  • MicroSD Express (PCIe NVMe over SD) is the widely leaked storage expansion standard for Nintendo Switch 2.
  • Early retail promotions offer up to 40% off standard list prices ahead of holiday supply constraints.

Retailers have initiated sharp price cuts on Samsung's next-generation P9 MicroSD Express memory lineup, dropping entry prices down to some of the lowest rates seen in months. The 256GB variant has been discounted to $47.99 from its standard $79.99 MSRP, while the flagship 512GB model has plunged to $99.99 at major retailers including Amazon and B&H Photo.

The sudden discount carries massive implications for console gamers tracking Nintendo Switch 2 hardware leaks. Supply-chain reports indicate Nintendo's upcoming successor will ditch standard UHS-I SD cards in favor of high-throughput MicroSD Express (PCIe Gen3 x1), achieving transfer rates up to 800 MB/s to eliminate open-world game loading bottlenecks without requiring proprietary SSD cartridges.

With global NAND flash memory pricing projected to tighten ahead of the holiday production ramp, acquiring high-speed MicroSD Express media at sub-$50 pricing offers gamers and tech hobbyists a cost-effective hedge before launch-day hardware bundles push secondary accessories into backorders.
