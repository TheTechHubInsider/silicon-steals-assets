Samsung Launches Luxury W27 Foldable: Gold Accents & Satellite Connectivity

⚡ Intel Brief TL;DR
  • Samsung officially unveils the China-exclusive W27 luxury foldable smartphone in partnership with China Telecom.
  • Features a black ceramic backplate, gold-armored aluminum hinge, and integrated two-way satellite communication.
  • Configured with 16GB RAM and 512GB UFS 4.0 storage starting at CNY 18,999 (~$2,850 USD).

Samsung has officially launched the W27 in China, continuing its annual high-luxury foldable partnership with carrier China Telecom. Built as an ultra-premium executive alternative to the mainline Galaxy Z Fold lineup, the W27 pairs cutting-edge flexible OLED engineering with bespoke craftsmanship, featuring a textured black ceramic backplate, gold-finished chamfered edges, and an engraved collector badge.

Under the hood, the W27 steps beyond consumer flagship specs by standardizing 16GB of LPDDR5X memory and 512GB of UFS 4.0 high-speed storage. Most notably for remote enterprise connectivity, Samsung has engineered direct hardware support for China's Tiantong satellite constellation, enabling two-way voice calls and emergency text dispatching when operating outside terrestrial cellular towers.

Available starting October 29 at CNY 18,999 (approximately $2,850 USD), the W27 is packaged as a complete luxury kit, including an S Pen Fold Edition with a matching gold sheath, a bespoke leather protective case, and a 65W GaN dual-port travel adapter.
