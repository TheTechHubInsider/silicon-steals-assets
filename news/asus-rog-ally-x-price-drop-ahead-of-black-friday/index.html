Handheld PC gamers looking for holiday deals do not have to wait until late November. Ahead of Black Friday, retail pricing on the acclaimed ASUS ROG Ally X has dropped to under $790 across authorized retailers, representing one of the first substantial price reductions on the refreshed Windows gaming flagship.

Unlike minor iterative revisions, the Ally X addresses every hardware bottleneck of the original generation. The internal battery capacity has doubled from 40Wh to a massive 80Wh, enabling 3 to 4 hours of demanding AAA gaming without tethering to a wall outlet. Thermals have also been overhauled with redesigned 77-blade fans that increase airflow by 24% while running quieter.

Under the hood, ASUS paired the 8-core AMD Ryzen Z1 Extreme APU with 24GB of blistering 7500MHz LPDDR5X memory. This memory expansion directly benefits shared VRAM allocation, allowing modern titles like Cyberpunk 2077 and Black Myth: Wukong to run smoothly without frame stuttering. Furthermore, the internal drive bay now supports standard M.2 2280 NVMe SSDs, drastically lowering the cost of storage upgrades.

With secondary USB-C ports (including USB4 Thunderbolt 4 support) and refined ergonomic grips with Hall-effect analog triggers, the ROG Ally X stands as the premier Windows handheld of the 2026 holiday season.
