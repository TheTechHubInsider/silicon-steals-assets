Nvidia RTX 5080 Blackwell Leaks: 16GB GDDR7 on 256-Bit Bus at 400W TDP

⚡ Intel Brief TL;DR
  • RTX 5080 expected to feature 10,752 CUDA cores on the GB203-400 die, mirroring RTX 4080 core counts.
  • VRAM capacity held at 16GB GDDR7, delivering up to 1,024 GB/s memory bandwidth over a 256-bit bus.
  • Board power consumption increases to an estimated 400W TDP, requiring robust 12V-2x6 power delivery.

Fresh board partner leaks reveal Nvidia's next-generation enthusiast desktop graphics card, the GeForce RTX 5080, based on the Blackwell GB203 silicon. While enthusiasts had hoped for an expansion to 24GB of VRAM, regulatory documentation confirms the card is configured with 16GB of ultra-fast GDDR7 memory.

Despite the unchanged 16GB capacity, memory throughput sees a massive generational leap. The transition to 28–32 Gbps GDDR7 modules pushes bandwidth beyond 1,000 GB/s over a 256-bit bus, outperforming the previous-generation RTX 4080 by nearly 42% in memory-intensive 4K textures and local AI inference batch processing.

Thermal requirements will be demanding: preliminary reference board specifications suggest a 400W TDP, up 80W from the RTX 4080. PC builders will need a native ATX 3.1 power supply with revised 12V-2x6 connectors to safely feed the increased transient power peaks.
