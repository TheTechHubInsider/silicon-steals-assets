## 5G Cellular Connectivity: Dual SIM, eSIM & Global Bands

Under the hood, the Mudi 7 is powered by the **Qualcomm Snapdragon X72 5G Modem-RF System (Dragonwing MBB Gen 3)**. Built on an ultra-efficient 4nm process, the X72 delivers downlink speeds up to **4.67 Gbps** and uplinks up to **1.25 Gbps** across Sub-6 GHz spectrum.

### Physical Dual Nano-SIM vs. Built-in eSIM Experience
The Mudi 7 combines **two physical Nano-SIM slots with an integrated digital eSIM chip**:
* **Physical Dual Nano-SIM:** Two spring-loaded trays beneath the battery bay support Dual-SIM Dual-Standby (DSDS), letting you keep your domestic carrier in Slot 1 and a local plastic SIM in Slot 2.
* **Integrated eSIM Management:** The built-in eSIM manager (`Network -> Cellular -> eSIM`) stores up to eight digital profiles. Users simply scan a QR code from providers like Airalo, Nomad, or Holafly. The profile downloads directly to the modem in under a minute, enabling immediate connectivity upon landing without opening the SIM tray.

### Regional Hardware Variants: NA vs. EU
Because global 5G frequencies differ, GL.iNet manufactures two dedicated regional hardware models:
* **North American Model (GL-E5800-NA):** Supports 5G NR bands n2, n5, n12, n14, n25, n30, n41, n48, n66, n71, n77 (C-Band), and n78. Optimized for T-Mobile, AT&T, and Verizon. (mmWave bands n258/n260/n261 are unsupported).
* **European / Global Model (GL-E5800-EU):** Supports 5G NR bands n1, n3, n5, n7, n8, n20, n28, n38, n40, n41, n77, and n78, tuned for European and Asian carriers.

> [!WARNING]
> **Cross-Continent Roaming Caveat:** While both versions maintain global 4G roaming, deploying an NA model in rural Europe can cause coverage drops. The NA variant omits European low-band frequencies like **5G Band n28 (700 MHz)** and **LTE Band 20 (800 MHz)**, meaning the router may struggle indoors or in rural districts where an EU model would thrive. Always buy the model matching your primary operating region.

### Is the GL.iNet Mudi 7 worth upgrading from the Mudi V2?
Yes, the GL.iNet Mudi 7 is a monumental upgrade over the Mudi V2. Upgrades include a Qualcomm Snapdragon 5G modem (vs. 4G LTE), Wi-Fi 7 tri-band speeds, a 2.5Gbps Multi-Gig port, built-in eSIM management, and a 12x boost in WireGuard VPN throughput (600+ Mbps compared to just 45 Mbps on V2). The upgrade completely eliminates network bottlenecks for demanding remote workflows.

### What is the real-world battery life of the GL.iNet Mudi 7?
Under active 5G cellular routing with WireGuard encryption enabled, the GL.iNet Mudi 7's 5,380mAh battery reliably delivers between 11 to 13.5 hours of continuous runtime. When operating strictly in Wi-Fi repeater mode with the 5G modem idle, battery endurance extends up to 16 hours on a single charge.

### Can the Mudi 7 bypass hotel and airport captive portals without leaking data?
Yes. The Mudi 7 includes GL.iNet’s captive portal detection tool. By connecting your smartphone or laptop through the Mudi 7 and temporarily suspending VPN enforcement via the touchscreen toggle, you can authenticate on the hotel splash screen. Once verified, the Mudi 7 automatically re-engages system-wide WireGuard encryption for all connected devices. The hotel network only ever sees a single authenticated MAC address.

### Does the Mudi 7 support both physical SIM cards and eSIM profiles?
Yes. The GL.iNet Mudi 7 features two physical Nano-SIM slots alongside an integrated digital eSIM chip. Travelers can store multiple international eSIM profiles directly on the router via the web interface and switch carrier profiles instantly upon landing without swapping physical cards or paying high cellular roaming fees.

### Can the Mudi 7 run continuously without its battery installed?
Yes. The Mudi 7 features a true mains battery-bypass mode. By removing the 5,380 mAh battery and plugging in a 30W+ USB-C PD wall charger, the router operates indefinitely. This prevents lithium-ion battery swelling and thermal wear when deployed as a continuous home backup or RV gateway.

### Does the Mudi 7 include an internal microSD card slot?
No. GL.iNet omitted the internal microSD card slot on the Mudi 7 to make room for internal 5G antennas and copper thermal heat spreaders. To share files locally across your network, you can connect an external flash drive or SSD to the 10 Gbps USB-C data port.
