## 4. Searcher Questions & FAQ Section (PAA Snippet Strategy)

### FAQ 1: Is the GL.iNet Mudi 7 worth upgrading from the Mudi V2?
> **Direct Snippet Answer (52 words):**  
> Yes, the GL.iNet Mudi 7 is a monumental upgrade over the Mudi V2. Upgrades include a Qualcomm Snapdragon 5G modem (vs. 4G LTE), Wi-Fi 7 tri-band speeds, a 2.5Gbps Multi-Gig port, built-in eSIM management, and a 12x boost in WireGuard VPN throughput (600+ Mbps compared to just 45 Mbps on V2).

### FAQ 2: What is the real-world battery life of the GL.iNet Mudi 7?
> **Direct Snippet Answer (48 words):**  
> Under active 5G cellular routing with WireGuard encryption enabled, the GL.iNet Mudi 7's 5,380mAh battery reliably delivers between 11 to 13.5 hours of continuous runtime. When operating strictly in Wi-Fi repeater mode with the 5G modem idle, battery endurance extends up to 16 hours on a single charge.

### FAQ 3: Can the Mudi 7 bypass hotel and airport captive portals without leaking data?
> **Direct Snippet Answer (53 words):**  
> Yes. The Mudi 7 includes GL.iNet’s captive portal detection tool. By connecting your smartphone or laptop through the Mudi 7 and temporarily suspending VPN enforcement via the physical toggle switch, you can authenticate on the hotel splash screen. Once verified, the Mudi 7 automatically re-engages system-wide WireGuard encryption for all connected devices.

### FAQ 4: Does the Mudi 7 support both physical SIM cards and eSIM profiles?
> **Direct Snippet Answer (51 words):**  
> Yes. The GL.iNet Mudi 7 features two physical Nano-SIM slots alongside an integrated digital eSIM chip. Travelers can store multiple international eSIM profiles directly on the router via the web interface and switch carrier profiles instantly upon landing without swapping physical cards or paying high cellular roaming fees.
