Editorial Standards & Methodology

Published: October 2026 | Silicon Steals Editorial Board

1. Complete Editorial Independence

Silicon Steals operates strictly under an independent editorial charter. We do not accept paid placement, sponsored reviews, or free review hardware with pre-conditions. Manufacturers and retailers have zero input, preview rights, or editorial veto over our reviews, scoring metrics, or buying recommendations.

2. Evaluation & Curation Methodology

Our buying guides and product evaluations are driven by a multi-layered verification framework:

  • Hardware & Architecture Verification: We cross-examine manufacturer whitepapers, FCC filings, teardown schematics, and sensor specifications to uncover real-world capabilities vs marketing hype.
  • Long-Term Ecosystem Viability: We prioritize products supporting open, subscription-free standards (such as Matter, Thread, RTSP/ONVIF, and local Home Assistant integration) over proprietary cloud paywalls.
  • Buyer Propensity Index (BPI): Our proprietary algorithm evaluates real market pricing, historical discount depth, verified user return rates, and true cost-per-feature value.

3. Honest Trade-offs & Limitations

Every piece of consumer hardware involves engineering compromises. Our editorial policy mandates that every single review highlights genuine downsides, quirks, and trade-offs. We explicitly delineate "Who Should Buy" and "Who Should Skip" for every reviewed item.

4. Corrections & Transparency

If an error, firmware change, or specification discrepancy is identified, we promptly review and update the respective article with a transparent change notice. Corrections can be submitted directly to editor@siliconsteals.com.
