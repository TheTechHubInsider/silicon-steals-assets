Editorial Standards & Testing Methodology

Silicon Steals is an independent consumer technology testing lab and real-time deal verification platform.

⚖️

1. Complete Editorial Independence & Zero Sponsored Bias

Silicon Steals operates strictly under an independent editorial charter.

Every device evaluated in our laboratory is either purchased at market retail or sourced through standard press loans with zero editorial conditions.

📉

2. Real-Time 90-Day Price Floor Verification

Most "deal" websites display artificial list prices created by sellers to fabricate fake 50% discounts.

  • 90-Day Street Floor Tracking: We continuously compare Amazon and retailer prices against rolling 90-day historical medians.
  • Deal Score Algorithm: Only products offering genuine street-price savings earn our verified "True Deal" designation.
  • Coupon & Lightning Deal Verification: We programmatically audit clippable coupons and lightning promotion codes before featuring any hardware offer.
🔬

3. 60-Second High-Density Hardware Teardowns

We reject 3,000-word filler reviews packed with unboxing prose and SEO fluff.

  • Thermal & Sustained Power Dissipation: Measuring real-world temperatures under sustained synthetic workloads.
  • Silicon & Controller Architecture: Inspecting NAND flash controllers, DRAM caches, Wi-Fi 7 chipset topologies, and USB-C PD power delivery negotiation.
  • Local Ecosystem Viability: Prioritizing open local control (Home Assistant, Matter, Thread, RTSP/ONVIF) over proprietary monthly cloud paywalls.
