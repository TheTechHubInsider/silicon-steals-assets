### 5. Honest Trade-offs & Limitations: What You Must Know Before Buying

Despite its overwhelming strengths, the RLK8-800B4 has specific physical and hardware limitations that every prospective buyer must understand before pulling cables through an attic. First, the B800 bullet cameras feature a fixed 4.0mm focal length lens with an **87° horizontal field of view**. While this wide angle is ideal for perimeter walls, double driveways, and backyards, there is no motorized optical zoom; identifying distant objects across an acre of open yard relies entirely on 4K digital cropping. Second, the audio hardware is strictly **one-way listen-only**. Each camera houses an ultra-sensitive microphone that captures clear environmental audio, but there is no integrated loudspeaker for two-way intercom talkback—meaning you cannot verbally warn off a trespasser from the camera itself like you can with a dedicated smart video doorbell. Third, the RLN8-410 NVR chassis utilizes an active **40mm internal cooling fan** that runs continuously to dissipate heat from the built-in 48V PoE switch and power supply. Emitting roughly **35 to 40 dB of ambient mechanical hum**, this NVR does not belong on a bedroom dresser or next to a whisper-quiet home office workstation; it belongs in a ventilated utility room, basement, structured wiring closet, or server rack. Finally, the bundled B800 cameras are system-dependent sub-devices engineered to route their streams directly through the Reolink NVR backplane rather than serving standalone web UIs.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can the Reolink RLK8-800B4 record 24/7 without internet or a cloud subscription?
Yes. The Reolink RLK8-800B4 operates completely offline without an active internet connection. All four 4K cameras stream continuous 24/7 video directly to the NVR's pre-installed 2TB hard drive through dedicated Ethernet cables. An internet connection is only needed if you wish to receive remote push notifications or view live camera feeds while away from your home Wi-Fi network.

### Does the Reolink RLK8-800B4 work natively with Home Assistant?
Yes. Reolink is an official "Works with Home Assistant" hardware partner. By simply entering the RLN8-410 NVR's local IP address and login credentials into Home Assistant, the system automatically exposes local RTSP video streams and instantaneous AI binary sensors (person, vehicle, and pet detection) directly to your smart home automations without cloud latency. For broader home automation ideas, explore our [local Home Assistant security automation guide](/guides/home-assistant-security-system-guide/).

### Can the bundled B800 4K cameras be used without the Reolink NVR?
No. The B800 bullet cameras included in the RLK8-800B4 package are system-dependent sub-devices engineered specifically to connect to the RLN8-410 NVR's PoE backplane. They lack independent web administration interfaces and onboard microSD card slots. If you require standalone Reolink PoE cameras that operate without an NVR, consider models like the Reolink RLC-810A or RLC-820A.

### How loud is the cooling fan on the Reolink RLN8-410 NVR, and where should it be installed?
The RLN8-410 NVR produces approximately 35 to 40 dB of steady ambient noise from its internal 40mm cooling fan. While necessary to dissipate heat generated by the 48V PoE power supply and hard drive, this mechanical hum can be irritating in quiet living areas. We strongly recommend locating the NVR in a ventilated basement, network closet, utility room, or equipment rack rather than a bedroom or quiet office desk.

### Can I expand the pre-installed 2TB hard drive on the Reolink RLK8-800B4?
Yes. The RLN8-410 NVR supports massive storage expansion up to a total of 12TB to 16TB. You can replace the internal 2TB drive with a 6TB or 8TB surveillance-rated SATA hard drive (such as a Western Digital Purple or Seagate SkyHawk), and add a second 6TB or 8TB drive via the external eSATA port on the rear chassis.
