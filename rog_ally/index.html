Frequently Asked Questions

Can you play Xbox Game Pass games on the ASUS ROG Ally?

Yes. Because the ROG Ally runs full native Windows 11 Home, you can download and play the entire PC Game Pass catalog directly, unlike SteamOS which requires clunky cloud streaming workarounds.

How long does the battery last in real-world gaming?

In light indie games or retro emulation at 10W-15W mode, the battery lasts approximately 3 to 4 hours. In demanding AAA titles running at 25W or 30W Turbo mode, battery life is between 60 and 90 minutes. Pairing with a 65W+ power bank is recommended for long flights.

Can you upgrade the internal SSD storage?

Yes. The ROG Ally uses a standard M.2 2230 PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD located beneath the rear shell. You can easily upgrade the included 512GB SSD to a 1TB or 2TB drive using a single screwdriver.

Does the 120Hz screen have FreeSync VRR support?

Yes. The display features AMD FreeSync Premium with a 48Hz-120Hz variable refresh rate range. This eliminates screen tearing and makes gameplay feel remarkably smooth even when framerates fluctuate.
