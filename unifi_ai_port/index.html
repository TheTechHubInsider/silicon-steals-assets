Upgrading an entire security camera deployment to access modern artificial intelligence features is traditionally expensive and disruptive. The Ubiquiti UniFi AI Port (UP-AI-Port) solves this dilemma by acting as an intelligent inline edge appliance. By plugging directly between your PoE network switch and any camera, it upgrades standard video streams with enterprise neural-network analytics.

The hardware processing inside the AI Port handles full 4K UHD video streams at 30 frames per second. It supports both native UniFi cameras and third-party ONVIF-compliant cameras. Video frames are analyzed locally in real time on-premises, completely eliminating the recurring monthly fees and cloud privacy risks associated with consumer camera subscriptions.

Where the AI Port truly shines is in its detection repertoire. It unlocks advanced License Plate Recognition (LPR), facial identification, vehicle classification (including make, type, and color), animal detection, and specific acoustic event monitoring (glass breaking, baby crying, carbon monoxide alarms). In testing, the detection latency is sub-second, allowing immediate Protect push notifications.

Hardware engineering is classic Ubiquiti: clean, rugged, and industrial. The unit features a solid aluminum body designed for wall, pole, or rack mounting, complete with an LED status ring. Because it incorporates an integrated PoE pass-through architecture, you run a single Ethernet drop from your PoE+ or PoE++ switch to the AI Port, and it powers both itself and the attached camera.

For homelabbers and businesses running UniFi Protect 5.1 or later, the AI Port integrates transparently into the UniFi Protect timeline. Detections appear alongside native UniFi AI camera feeds, allowing instant chronological filtering by license plate, person, or vehicle attribute.

At its regular $199.00 MSRP (and particularly at its current $129.99 deal price), the UniFi AI Port is an extraordinary bargain for anyone wanting to supercharge existing camera infrastructure without discarding perfectly functional optics.
