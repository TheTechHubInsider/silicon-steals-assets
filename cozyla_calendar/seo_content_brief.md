## 4. Searcher Questions & FAQ Section (PAA Snippet Strategy)

### FAQ 1: Does the Cozyla Calendar require a monthly or annual subscription?
> **Direct Snippet Answer (52 words):**  
> No, the Cozyla Calendar does not require a subscription to access its core features. Unlike the Skylight Calendar, which charges $79/year for full functionality, Cozyla provides lifetime access to two-way calendar sync, chore and reward charts, meal planning, grocery lists, and photo frame display completely free of recurring fees.

### FAQ 2: How does the Cozyla Calendar sync with Google, Apple, and Outlook calendars?
> **Direct Snippet Answer (51 words):**  
> The Cozyla Calendar syncs seamlessly via secure cloud integration using your Google, Apple iCloud, or Microsoft Outlook accounts. Once connected in the Cozyla mobile app or on-screen settings, events update automatically with two-way synchronization, meaning changes made on your smartphone appear on the wall display within seconds, and vice versa.

### FAQ 3: What is the difference between Cozyla Calendar and Skylight Calendar?
> **Direct Snippet Answer (54 words):**  
> The main difference lies in screen size, subscription costs, and software flexibility. Cozyla offers larger screen options (up to 32 inches) with zero mandatory subscriptions and full Google Play app support. Skylight is limited to smaller displays (10" to 15"), requires a $79/year subscription for chore charts and meal planners, and runs closed software.

### FAQ 4: Can you hang the Cozyla Calendar on the wall and hide the power cord?
> **Direct Snippet Answer (50 words):**  
> Yes. Cozyla calendars include standard VESA mounting points and a custom flush wall-mounting bracket. To conceal the power cord, homeowners can either run the low-voltage DC cable through an in-wall cable conduit kit or install a recessed power outlet directly behind the screen for a completely floating, cable-free appearance.
