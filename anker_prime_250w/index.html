The Hardware Dilemma & Market Context

In an era where high-performance laptops like the 16-inch MacBook Pro and workstation PCs demand high-wattage USB-C Power Delivery 3.1 charging, traditional power banks have struggled to keep pace. The Anker Prime 27,650mAh Power Bank (Model A1340) directly resolves this bottleneck, delivering an unprecedented 250W of combined multi-port output in a sleek, soda-can-sized silhouette engineered to sit right at the legal limit of commercial aviation carry-on guidelines.

Power Delivery & Internal Architecture

The battery architecture packs a dense 27,650mAh reserve totaling 99.54 watt-hours, engineered precisely under the strict 100Wh threshold mandated by the FAA, TSA, and international airline regulators for carry-on luggage. Power delivery is powered by advanced PowerIQ 3.0 and native USB PD 3.1 Extended Power Range (EPR), allowing a single USB-C port to output an immense 140W (28V/5A).

Charge Velocity & Efficiency Metrics

Recharge velocity is where the Anker Prime establishes total category dominance. Unlike conventional high-capacity battery packs that require four to six hours to replenish, the Prime 27,650mAh supports dual-port input up to 170W.

Port Density & Real-World Utility

What elevates the user experience from mere utility to luxury hardware is the vivid front-facing smart color display. Rather than relying on ambiguous blinking indicator LEDs, the dynamic UI visualizes real-time input wattage, output wattage per individual port, remaining battery percentage, estimated time to empty, and overall internal battery health cycles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you take the Anker Prime 27,650mAh on an airplane?

Yes. The FAA and TSA permit lithium-ion battery banks with a capacity of 100 watt-hours (Wh) or less in carry-on baggage.

Can it charge two laptops simultaneously?

Yes. The dual USB-C ports can deliver up to 140W and 100W concurrently, allowing you to fast-charge two high-demand USB-C laptops at full operational speed.

How fast does it recharge from 0%?

Using a single 140W USB-C charger, it reaches 100% in about 52 minutes. Using dual-port 170W input (e.g. two fast chargers or the optional charging base), it reaches 100% in just 37 minutes.

What is the Bluetooth app connection used for?

The Anker companion app allows you to view real-time battery diagnostics, set battery health conservation charge thresholds, adjust screen sleep timeout, and trigger a locate chime.
